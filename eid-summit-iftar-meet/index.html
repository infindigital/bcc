🌙 Eid Summit & Iftar Meet

BCC proudly organizes annual Eid Summit and Iftar Meet programs that bring together hundreds of Beary community members in Bengaluru for meaningful celebrations. These distinguished gatherings serve as platforms for unity, cultural connection, and strengthening bonds across generations.

Highlights:

  • Community-wide Iftar gatherings promoting brotherhood and unity
  • Recognition and felicitation of Beary achievers from various fields
  • Networking opportunities for professionals, students, and families
  • Cultural programs celebrating our rich Islamic and Beary heritage
