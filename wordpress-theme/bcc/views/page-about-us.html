About BCC

Empowering Communities, Transforming Lives

At the Bearys Central Committee (BCC), we believe that a strong community is the foundation of a thriving society. For years, we have stood as a pillar of support for the Beary community and beyond, providing essential services that touch lives during their most critical moments.

Rooted in the rich cultural heritage of the Beary community and guided by timeless values of service and solidarity, BCC has evolved into a trusted force for positive change across Greater Bangalore. Our journey began with a simple yet powerful commitment: to ensure that no member of our community faces life’s challenges alone. Today, we continue that legacy through dedicated volunteers, passionate leaders, and countless supporters who share our vision of a more compassionate and equitable society.

BEARYS

“Long before the age of empires, the monsoon winds brought more than just rain to Mangalore – they brought the first seeds of Islam. In 644 AD, the legendary Malik Deenar arrived at the Bunder coast. He didn’t come as a conqueror, but as a trader and a man of faith. This peaceful arrival laid the foundation for a community that would become the bridge between the East and the West.”

BEARY LANGUAGE - A LANGUAGE OF HARMONY

"The word ‘Beary’ is derived from the Tulu word Byara, meaning trade. For over 1,200 years, the Beary community has spoken a unique tongue - Beary Bashe (Beary Language). It is a linguistic mosaic, blending the grammar of Tulu, the vocabulary of Malayalam, and the soul of Arabic. It is a language born on the docks, refined in the marketplace, and preserved in the home."

THE BEARYS CENTRAL COMMITTEE, BENGALURU

The Bearys Central Committee Bengaluru (R) in Short BCC, represents the modern, organized face of the Beary community in Karnataka’s capital. While the community has roots in the coast, their migration to Bangalore for trade and education led to the formation of this central body to protect their interests and preserve their identity.

Aims & Objectives

Unity

Providing a single platform for all Bearys in Bangalore, regardless of their political or local affiliations.

Political Empowerment

Providing a single platform for all Bearys in Bangalore, regardless of their political or local affiliations.

Legal & Professional Aid:

Offering a support system for Beary individuals facing injustice in their workplaces or business ventures.

Cultural Preservation:

Ensuring that the Beary Bashe (language) and unique coastal traditions do not get lost in the urban sprawl.

Origin & Establishment

Though Beary organizations existed in Bangalore for decades, the Bearys Central Committee (BCC) Bengaluru was formally established recently in the year 2024, the holy month of Ramadan to act as a unified umbrella for the Beary Community of Bengaluru . It was formed by professionals, businessmen, and residents who migrated from the coastal regions to settle in the city.

Key Activities & Achievements

The Bearys Central Committee, Bengaluru (BCC) is widely recognized for its high-impact, grassroots initiatives that successfully blend social responsibility, civic engagement, education, and cultural pride. Its activities reflect the evolution of the Beary community from a coastal trading society to an organized and influential urban presence in India’s technology capital.
